# Round 7 — Provider Portal UX

## UX objective

The portal should primarily answer:

**What does the provider need to do next?**

Actionability takes priority over decorative analytics.

## Primary navigation

Recommended top-level areas:

- Home
- Opportunities
- Proposals
- Engagements
- Work
- Calendar
- Services
- Earnings
- Invoices
- Documents
- Profile
- Messages
- Notifications
- Support
- Settings

## Dashboard

Show:

- Action center
- Active engagements
- Upcoming deadlines
- Recommended opportunities
- Current availability/capacity
- Earnings summary
- Pending approvals
- Invoiceable amount
- Outstanding invoices/payments
- Profile/compliance issues

## Action center

Aggregate actionable items such as:

- Complete onboarding
- Sign document
- Respond to invitation
- Revise proposal
- Accept offer
- Submit deliverable
- Correct deliverable
- Submit timesheet
- Update expiring verification
- Submit invoice
- Respond to dispute/change request

## Opportunities

Provide:

- Recommended opportunities
- Eligible opportunities
- Invitations
- Saved opportunities
- Search/filter
- Opportunity detail
- Application/proposal status

## Proposals

Provide list/detail views with states, versions, messages, revision requests, and final offer.

## Engagements

Each engagement gets an operational workspace covering:

- Overview
- Scope
- Milestones
- Tasks
- Deliverables
- Time/timesheets
- Change requests
- Documents
- Communication
- Commercial terms
- Earnings
- Activity

## Work queue

Cross-engagement work view ordered by urgency and due date.

## Calendar

Combine:

- Interviews
- Engagement dates
- Milestones
- Deadlines
- Planned absence
- Availability
- Meetings

## Capacity

Show:

- Total weekly capacity
- Current allocation
- Reserved future allocation
- Planned absence
- Available capacity

## Earnings

Show:

- Earned
- Pending approval
- Invoiceable
- Invoiced
- Awaiting payment
- Paid

No client pricing or SDK margin.

## Services

Provider can create/edit service drafts and submit them for SDK approval.

## Profile

Manage profile, skills, portfolio, availability, business information, verification, and profile completeness.

## Documents

Central provider document center for contracts, NDAs, tax/compliance, invoices, payment evidence, and engagement documents.

## Messages

Use contextual threads rather than a generic chat-only product.

## Notifications

Provide in-app notification center with configurable email preferences.

## Support

Provider can raise support cases and see status/history.

## Settings

Include account, security, notification, availability, billing/payout, privacy, and integrations where relevant.

## Responsive UX

All core provider workflows must work on mobile/responsive layouts.

## Accessibility

Meet modern accessibility expectations: keyboard usability, semantic controls, focus states, labels, readable contrast, screen-reader-friendly status communication.

## Error and empty states

Design explicit empty/loading/error/permission states for all major pages.

## Activity history

Expose provider-visible activity history for important workflow actions without revealing internal SDK-only data.
