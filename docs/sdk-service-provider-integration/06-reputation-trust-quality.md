# Round 6 — Reputation, Trust & Quality

## Reputation layers

Maintain:

- Internal SDK reputation
- Client-visible rating

They are related but not identical.

## Internal scoring

Track:

- Delivery quality
- Reliability
- Communication
- Timeliness
- Client satisfaction
- SDK manager satisfaction
- Scope discipline
- Responsiveness
- Rework
- Disputes
- Completion rate
- Repeat-client demand

Keep historical scores.

## Client reviews

Allow eligible clients to rate:

- Quality
- Communication
- Timeliness
- Professionalism
- Overall satisfaction

Support written feedback and provider responses.

## Provider feedback on clients

Collect private provider feedback for SDK operational use.

## Review eligibility

Only after meaningful engagement activity.

## Moderation

Support report, moderation, hiding, reasons, appeal, and audit history.

## Verified skills

Statuses:

`Declared → Reviewed → Verified → Expired/Revoked`

Evidence may come from portfolio, certification, assessment, SDK engagement, or manual review.

## Badges

Use meaningful badges only, including:

- Verified Provider
- Identity Verified
- Business Verified
- Skill Verified
- Preferred Provider
- High Reliability
- Repeat Client Choice

## Internal tiers

`New → Established → Preferred → Strategic`

## Provider health

`Healthy → Watch → At Risk → Restricted → Suspended → Offboarding → Archived`

## Risk signals

Examples:

- Repeated lateness
- Repeated disputes
- Suspicious invoice changes
- Payout-detail changes
- Identity/business mismatch
- Client complaints
- Availability inconsistencies
- Repeated withdrawals

Signals trigger review, not automatic punishment.

## Warnings

Support informal, formal, and final warnings with evidence, required corrective action, review date, and resolution.

## Restrictions

Capability-specific restrictions may block applications, service publishing, engagement acceptance, proposals, or payout changes.

## Suspension

`Pending Review → Suspended → Reinstated/Offboarded`

Do not remove access to legally required historical documents.

## Offboarding

Support provider-initiated and SDK-initiated exits with engagement review, financial reconciliation, contract handling, final payments, access reduction, retention, and archival.

## Temporary inactivity

Separate from offboarding.

## Incidents, complaints, disputes

Keep distinct:

- Complaint = dissatisfaction/report
- Incident = operational/compliance event
- Dispute = contested contractual/work/financial matter

They may reference one another.

## Appeals

Allow appeals for important trust decisions.

## Internal notes

Permission-scoped notes such as general, finance, compliance, management.

## Matching integration

Reputation can influence matching through configurable weights.

## Manual overrides

Require authorization, reason, actor, timestamp, previous value, and optional expiry.

## Trust invariants

- Internal score != public rating
- Complaint != proven misconduct
- Risk signal != guilt
- Suspended != deleted
- Reputation != one static number
- Verified skill != permanent verification
- Historical feedback is not overwritten
